# Responsive Design Best Practices

Learn how to build websites that look great on any device.

## Mobile First

Always design for mobile first. Start with a simple layout for small screens, then progressively enhance for larger screens. This approach ensures your site works everywhere.

## Breakpoints

Use meaningful breakpoints rather than device-specific ones. Focus on where your content naturally needs to reflow rather than targeting specific devices.
