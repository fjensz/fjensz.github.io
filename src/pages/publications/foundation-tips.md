# Foundation Tips

Here are some tips for working with Foundation for Sites:

- Use the XY Grid for flexible layouts
- Leverage the callout component for highlighted content
- Take advantage of the built-in responsive design

## Master the XY Grid

The XY Grid is one of Foundation's most powerful features. It allows you to create complex layouts with ease. Unlike the traditional float-based grid, the XY Grid uses flexbox for better alignment and spacing control.

## Callout Components

Callouts are perfect for highlighting important information. They come in several varieties including default, warning, and success. Use them sparingly to draw attention to key points without overwhelming your users.
